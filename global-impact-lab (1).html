How the Linkage Method Works

Every case study in this lab follows the same six-step analyst discipline.

StepWhat You DoSkill Built
1. The TriggerIdentify the global/political event and why it happenedContextual awareness
2. The ChainFollow the transmission: global → India → sector → companyCausal reasoning
3. The NumbersRead actual before/after data — prices, indices, currency, financialsQuantitative analysis
4. Trace-the-NumberCalculate one link in the chain yourselfApplied financial math
5. Company VerdictJudge how one real company's numbers moved, and whyEquity research judgement
6. Simulate the PriceFeed in your own sales/cost assumptions and watch profit, EPS and P/E drive the share priceValuation mechanics
